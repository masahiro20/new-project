## 2. Free tier

We intend Kotomark to stay free for:

- **individuals** using it on their own behalf (hobbyists, freelancers, solo developers), paid work included —
  but not on behalf of an employer or client organization that is outside the free tier;
- **open-source projects** (projects whose source is published under an OSI-approved license), for use on that project;
- **organizations with 3 or fewer employees** (counting the whole organization, including affiliates it controls or is controlled by).

## 3. Paid plans

- Other organizations are expected to use a **paid plan** once paid plans are available. A paid plan is
  provided under a **separate commercial agreement** with the Kotomark team and comes with a license key
  and/or subscription.
- Paid-plan features may be unlocked by a license key. ELv2 does not allow circumventing license key
  functionality.
- **Planned pricing** (not yet in effect; may change before launch):

  | Plan | Who | Planned price |
  |---|---|---|

  | Studio | Any organization | US$49/month for up to 5 seats, +US$9/month per extra seat. Annual billing: 2 months free (pay for 10). JPY guide ≈ ¥7,800/month. Includes the hosted MCP server and shared glossaries. |

  Paid plans are not on sale yet.

## 4. Preview period

**Until paid plans launch, everyone may use Kotomark free of charge as a "preview".** No payment is owed
for use during the preview. When paid plans launch we will announce pricing in advance; use before that date
stays free.
