# CrazyGames submission text (GRANDSTRIDE)

## Title
GRANDSTRIDE

## Short description (one line)
Pilot a 28-meter four-legged war machine from the cockpit. Hold the seawall, upgrade your mech and take down the shell lords.

## Description
The sea has swallowed the lower city. The Ashshell are climbing the seawall. You are strapped into the saddle of HEKATON TYPE-04, a four-legged war machine.

GRANDSTRIDE is a first-person cockpit mech action game. You never leave the saddle: read the gauges, heed the warning lights, keep four legs planted and hold the line.

- 3 missions, 3 bosses: the Shell Lord, the Twinshell and the Maelshell.
- Four legs, four grip gauges. Turning, landing and taking hits wear them down; when a leg loses grip the machine slows and leans.
- Build resonance with a steady rhythm to reach OVERBEAT, but watch the core temperature.
- Spend PARTS in the hangar to upgrade legs, core and armor.
- Medals, HARD mode, daily goals and an assist mode if a mission keeps beating you.
- Every sound is synthesized live. Your progress is saved automatically.

## How to play (instructions)
Desktop: W / S move, A / D turn, mouse to aim, left click to fire, Shift to dash, Space to boost (Space + A / D to sidestep), Esc or P to pause.
Mobile: left stick to move and turn, drag on the right side to look, FIRE / BOOST / DASH buttons.

Sci-fi combat against insect-like machines. No blood, no human characters harmed, no gambling, no chat, no external links.

## Notes for the review team
- Ads: midgame only, requested after the player presses a button on the mission result screen (never during a mission, in menus or on the title). Audio is muted while an ad plays. If an ad is unavailable the game simply continues.
- Save data uses the SDK Data module.
- AI disclosure: this game was made with extensive use of generative AI (Claude) under the direction of Team GRANDSTRIDE. The code, the text and the design documents were drafted by AI and then play-tested, edited and approved by the team. No AI image-generation models and no AI-generated audio files are used: visuals are drawn at runtime by AI-written three.js code and all sound is synthesized live with the Web Audio API.
